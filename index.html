Our Story

Founded in 2018, Noir was born from a desire to create a dining experience that transcends the ordinary. Every dish tells a story — of heritage, innovation, and an unwavering commitment to excellence.

Our chefs bring together classical techniques and bold modern flavors, sourcing only the finest seasonal ingredients to craft menus that surprise and delight.
